How I work

FROM BRIEF
TO PRINT
  1. 01

    First, we talk

    Tell me about your brand, who you want to reach and where the design will end up. I ask a lot of questions. It saves us both time later.

  2. 02

    Then I sketch

    A few rough ideas on the table, nothing polished yet. You tell me what feels right and we pick one direction.

  3. 03

    I design it

    Now I build it properly: type, color, layout, motion. I make sure it looks like the same brand everywhere it shows up.

  4. 04

    You review

    You tell me what to change and I change it. I also check every file for print and for each platform (sizes, colors, bleed), so nothing goes wrong later.

  5. 05

    Cleared for print

    The final files land in your inbox, ready to post or send to the printer. Done, stamped, and you're good to go.
